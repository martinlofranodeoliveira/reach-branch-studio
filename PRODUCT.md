## Stack

## Users

An open-source portfolio tool for students, roboticists, and engineers inspecting 3-revolute-joint arm kinematics, inverse kinematics branches, joint limits, and reachability.

## Product Purpose

A React 3D / Three.js educational serial manipulator workbench explaining analytic forward and inverse kinematics, elbow branches, signed radial alternatives, joint-limit rejection, and singularities in linked 3D and 2D views.
